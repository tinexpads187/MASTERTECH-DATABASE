MasterTech Database
We used mastertech_db, a MariaDB database, to manage and organize the data for our Computer and Electronics Shop Management System. It handles our system's core operations, including user and customer management, product and inventory tracking, sales and payment transactions, repair services and appointments, supplier records, returns, discounts, and business expenses.
We implemented relational tables with primary and foreign key constraints to maintain data integrity, ensure organized data storage, and establish relationships between different system modules.
